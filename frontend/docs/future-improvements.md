# Frontend future improvements

Tracked ideas for later frontend work. Not scheduled — add items here when we defer a feature instead of scattering notes across docs.

## Energy and profiles

- **Alternative energy model: EFSA (Henry + PAL)** — Let users choose between the current **Mifflin–St Jeor + US activity factors** stack and the EU reference stack: **Henry (2005) REE** × **EFSA PAL** (1.4 / 1.6 / 1.8 / 2.0). Useful for parity with [EFSA DRV tables](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/3005) or when users expect “low active” sedentary at 1.4 rather than US 1.2.

## Recipes and cooking

- **Goal: lose / maintain / gain** — Offset TDEE with a user-chosen calorie surplus or deficit.
- **Search recipes by a list of ingredients** — User names several foods they have (or want to use). Two match modes: **(a)** the recipe’s ingredients are a *subset* of that list (cookable from what you have, no extras), **(b)** the recipe *contains all* of those ingredients (must use everything named). Home today only filters by recipe name and meal type.

## Profiles

- **Unspecified sex** — Optional midpoint BMR intercept when sex is not provided.
- **Body-fat %** — Katch–McArdle or similar when composition data is available.

## Locale and display

- **kJ beside kcal** — Show energy in kJ where locale prefers it.
